Workflow

A clear development path.

The objective is to reduce uncertainty before batch production and keep design intent aligned with structure, material and installation conditions.

01 · Brief

Drawing, rendering, photo, dimensions, target finish, quantity and installation conditions.

02 · Engineering

Review structure, materials, light source, mounting method and production feasibility.

03 · Sample

Prototype development and confirmation of appearance, size and key details.

04 · Production

Batch manufacturing, assembly, inspection, packing and shipment coordination.
