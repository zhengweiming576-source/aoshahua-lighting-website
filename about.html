About AOSHAHUA

A production-oriented lighting partner from Jiangmen.

Jiangmen AOSHAHUA Lighting Co., Ltd. focuses on the design, production and sale of hotel and decorative lighting, supported by more than 20 years of industry experience.

Company Positioning

Standard products + flexible customization.

Our core product direction includes hotel guestroom lighting, natural alabaster lighting, modern crystal chandeliers and project-custom fixtures, alongside wall lamps, table lamps, floor lamps and pendants.

We serve hospitality, apartments, villas and commercial projects, supporting sample development, OEM/ODM, small-batch production and volume delivery.
